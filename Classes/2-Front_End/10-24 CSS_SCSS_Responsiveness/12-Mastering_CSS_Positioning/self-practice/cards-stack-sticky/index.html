Characters in Spiderman - Brand New Day

Spider-Man

Peter Parker is a young superhero who uses his spider-like abilities to protect New York City while struggling with life on his own.

MJ

Michelle "MJ" Jones-Watson is Peter Parker's former girlfriend, who has moved on with her life but remains an important part of Peter's story.

Jean Grey

Jean Grey is a powerful mutant with extraordinary telepathic abilities, bringing the X-Men's mutant world into Spider-Man's story.

Hulk

Bruce Banner is a brilliant scientist whose experiments transformed him into the incredibly powerful Hulk.

Punisher

Frank Castle is a ruthless vigilante who uses military skills and brutal methods to fight criminals and those he considers enemies.

Yelena Belova

Yelena Belova is a highly trained spy and former Black Widow who brings her experience, combat skills, and sharp attitude to the story.

Ned Leeds

Ned Leeds is Peter's former best friend, whose connection with Peter remains complicated after the events that changed their lives.

Scorpion

Mac Gargan is a dangerous criminal who becomes the armored villain known as Scorpion and has a long-standing grudge against Spider-Man.
